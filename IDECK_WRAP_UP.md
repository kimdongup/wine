# iDeck investigation closed — 2026-11-09 HST

The user ended the iDeck Steam64 investigation after the attempts failed to
meet its end-to-end acceptance criteria. This fork preserves experimental
changes; it is not a validated Steam64 release or an upstream submission.

The final working changes cover host address-space sizing and debugger,
event, handle, named-pipe and process behavior/diagnostics. They are retained
as investigation state. A successful full rebuild, complete IPC correctness,
interactive Steam login and end-to-end stability are not established here.

See iDeck's `docs/STEAM64_FINAL_WRAP_UP_2026-11-09.md` for scope, retained
